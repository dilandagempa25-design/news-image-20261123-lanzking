# The Day in News — Monday 23 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun contest **news-image-20261123**
("Sum up 23 November's world news in one image"), built for the
account **@lanzking**.

## What it is

A single 1024×1024 breaking-news poster, drawn entirely in code.
A dated banner ("MONDAY — THE DAY IN NEWS / 23 NOVEMBER 2026")
sits over a 2×4 grid of story tiles. Each tile has a small icon
drawn with PIL primitives, a bold headline and a one-line summary,
so a viewer who missed the day can tell what happened from the
image alone:

1. **COP31 talks collapse** — the Antalya climate summit ends with
   no deal; 130+ nations reject a draft on fossil fuels.
2. **Warship sunk by sea drones** — Ukraine's drones sink Russia's
   *Merkury* at Benghazi; the UN calls for a probe.
3. **Strikes kill 12** — Russian strikes hit the Kyiv region; a boy
   of 7 is pulled alive from the rubble.
4. **Nvidia shares soar 30%** — Jensen Huang back as CEO; the US
   blacklists Megaspeed over AI chips.
5. **Delta ground stop** — a load-system crash delays 1,300 flights
   in Atlanta on Thanksgiving eve.
6. **GTA VI smashes records** — a $3.79B day-one haul, the biggest
   in entertainment history.
7. **Ebola teams trapped** — fighting in Ituri (DR Congo) strands
   responders near a hospital, per WHO.
8. **UK to host 2035 World Cup** — FIFA confirms the women's
   tournament; Alcaraz wins his first ATP Finals title.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Requires Python 3 and Pillow, plus the DejaVu Sans fonts. Writes
`entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG, well under 1 MiB) next to `main.py`.
Everything — layout, icons and text — is generated by the script;
there are no external assets.
